What we found / 1 of 3

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

01

The offer leaves costly questions.

The offer lists scan and placement, but leaves finished-tooth inclusions, eligibility and the countdown deadline unclear. Patients may abandon the inquiry or arrive expecting more. See the implant page ↗

02

Strong claims need visible support.

Expertise and success-rate comparisons lack an explanation beside them. Specific credentials build more trust than claims patients cannot check. No legal or clinical violation is concluded. See the implant claims ↗

03

The disclaimer weakens patient confidence.

It says testimonials may come from people who are not actual patients. That wording can make implant shoppers doubt proof instead of booking. No particular testimonial is shown to be false. Read the disclaimer ↗

What we found / 2 of 3

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

04

Give the site a clear search role.

Inspected landing pages lack descriptions; the homepage blocks indexing. If it should attract local search traffic, that setting works against it. Paid-ad pages may intentionally be excluded. See the landing site ↗

05

18.5 seconds in the phone test.

October 20’s landing-homepage slow 4G simulation measured this delay. Patients may leave before seeing your doctors or offer. Lab LCP, not every visit; no real-visitor dataset. See the phone test ↗

What we found / 3 of 3

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

06

Requests, offers or calls?

Request Appointment, Save Offer for Later, Book Online and call options compete. Unclear next steps can stop an interested patient from sending an inquiry. No form was submitted. See the request routes ↗

07

Connect calls to campaigns.

Multiple phone routes and two Meta Pixel identifiers need one routing and measurement check. If unclear, ad decisions can miss the consultations that matter. No duplicate events or failed routing established. See the landing site ↗

How we would help / 1 of 2

HERE’S THE FIX. The work we’d do.

01 + 02

Make the offer easy to understand.

Spell out inclusions, exclusions, eligibility and any real deadline, including a clear insurance notice. Replace broad comparisons with verified, practice-approved credentials and evidence.

03

Put genuine patient proof in context.

Review testimonial permissions and source attribution. Clarify the disclaimer, link current review profiles and pair consented case stories with accurate treatment context.

04

Give each site a clear job.

Decide which pages serve paid ads and which should rank locally. Add useful descriptions and local treatment content; change indexing only where it matches the agreed role.

How we would help / 2 of 2

HERE’S THE FIX. The work we’d do.

05

Build a faster consultation page.

Prioritize the first image and critical styles. Reduce unnecessary scripts, retest the same phone profile and preserve the full offer and appointment journey.

06 + 07

One clear, measured request path.

Clarify requests and phone routing. Verify tag ownership and report aggregate consultation counts, without sending patient or treatment details to ad platforms.
