Finding 1 of 3

Clear up the review wording.

What we saw

The disclaimer says testimonials may not come from patients. That does not prove any review is false.

Why it matters

Readers may be unsure which stories they can trust.

Our fix

Check each story’s source. Use clear, accurate wording and links to the original reviews.

Finding 2 of 3

Explain the whole treatment.

What we saw

Ads mention a consult, 3D scan and implant placement. Patients still need the finished tooth explained.

Why it matters

Patients may ask for something different from what is offered.

Our fix

Show what is included, what is extra and who qualifies. Match both messages.

Finding 3 of 3

Bring the main page in sooner.

What we saw

Google’s October 20 phone simulation took 18.5 seconds to show the main content. No real-visitor data was available.

Why it matters

A patient may leave after clicking an ad.

Our fix

Load key images sooner and remove extra scripts. Retest the appointment route.
